# About the Hub

The Macro Forecast Hub brings together researchers, central banks, and industry
to compare probabilistic forecasts of U.S. macroeconomic indicators using open
data and a shared evaluation framework.

- **12 configured indicators**, with four currently visualized and scored.
- **1–24 month horizons**, with five quantiles: 0.05, 0.10, 0.50, 0.90, 0.95.
- **Monthly registration** on the 17th, during a 24-hour Eastern Time window.
- **Hub ensemble** combining submitted forecasts by their median.

The dashboards include historical backfills and revised observations. Historical
accuracy does not establish prospective, first-release performance.

## Inspiration

The project adapts version-controlled submissions, automated validation, and
ensemble aggregation from the [European COVID-19 Forecast Hub](https://github.com/european-modelling-hubs/RespiCast-Covid19),
[US COVID-19 Forecast Hub](https://github.com/reichlab/covid19-forecast-hub), and
[Hubverse](https://hubverse.io/) to macroeconomic forecasting.
